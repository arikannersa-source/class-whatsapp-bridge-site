Class WhatsApp Bridge

WhatsApp group updates, delivered by SMS.

A small messaging service operated by Ari Kanner for people who do not use WhatsApp. People are enrolled only after they explicitly agree to receive relayed messages from selected groups.

What messages are sent?

Messages posted in WhatsApp groups selected for you may be relayed to your mobile phone by SMS. Relayed messages can include the original sender's display name and message text.

This program is not used for advertising or promotional marketing.

Important SMS information

  • Message frequency varies based on activity in the groups you choose.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out.
  • Reply HELP for help.

Consent to receive SMS group-message relays

Use this page to create a written consent receipt. This website does not transmit your phone number to a server. After generating the receipt, give or show it to the service operator who invited you. The operator will add your number only after receiving your consent.

Save, copy, or show this receipt to the service operator. The operator should retain a record of your consent.

Opting out or getting help

You can revoke consent at any time by replying STOP to a program text. Reply HELP for help, or contact the person who enrolled you in the service.
